1. Install **QQQ AI Visibility Checker** from the Registry section of your EmDash admin and accept the single permission: network requests to `qqq.ee`.
2. Open the dashboard. The **AI visibility** widget shows "Not checked yet". Click **Check now**.
3. If your site runs on a preview or local address, open the plugin settings and enter your public domain, for example `example.com`.
4. To let your site's AI agent use the checker, enable the plugin's MCP tools in the admin.

The plugin re-checks the site once a week. To see whether AI assistants actually name your company, run the free [full AI answer check on qqq.ee](https://qqq.ee/en/ai-visibility-checker/).
